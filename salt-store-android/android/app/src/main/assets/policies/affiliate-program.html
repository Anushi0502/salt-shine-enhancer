Affiliate program

SALT welcomes partnership inquiries from creators, publishers, and community partners who want to share products they genuinely recommend.

Program overview

Affiliate opportunities are reviewed case by case. Approved partners may receive tracking links, campaign details, and promotional guidance from the team.

Who it is for

Creators, reviewers, and community members who align with the store's product style and want to introduce useful products to their audience.

How to inquire

Use the contact support card in the app or email support@saltonlinestore.com with your name, platform, and partnership idea.

Notes

Affiliate participation is subject to approval and may change over time based on campaign fit and store needs.
