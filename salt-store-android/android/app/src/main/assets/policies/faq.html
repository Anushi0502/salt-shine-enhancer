Frequently asked questions

How does checkout work?
Add items to cart and continue checkout inside the app. The secure Shopify flow opens in the native checkout wrapper.

Is my cart saved?
Yes. Cart state is stored on this device so you can return and finish checkout later.

How do returns work?
Returns and refunds follow the store policy. Open the returns card in the support center for details.

How can I contact SALT?
Use the contact support card to open the store contact details and official social channels.

Why do some pages open in the app while checkout stays separate?
Product, support, and policy surfaces stay native. Checkout uses Shopify directly so pricing, cart contents, and payment handoff stay live.
