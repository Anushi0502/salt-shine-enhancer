Return Policy

We want you to love your purchase. Please read the return policy below:

1. Order Cancellations

Orders can be canceled within 24 hours of placement, before they ship. After processing or shipment, cancellations are not possible.

2. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, contact us within 7 days of receiving your order. Please include a photo or video so we can verify the issue.

After verification, we will provide a replacement or issue a refund without requiring the item to be returned.

3. Change of Mind

We do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or buyer's remorse. Please review product details carefully before purchasing.

4. Non-Returnable Items

Custom products, clearance items, and products marked non-returnable on their product page are not eligible for return.

5. Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error, such as an incorrect or defective item.

6. Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the returned item is received and confirmed to be in its original, unused condition. Shipping fees are non-refundable. Partial refunds may be issued at our discretion.

7. Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received your refund, please check with your bank or card provider first. If you still need help, contact help@saltonlinestore.com.

8. Final Sale Items

Clearance and sale items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.

9. How to Initiate a Return

Email help@saltonlinestore.com with your order number and a description of the issue, and we will send next steps.

10. Shipping Times and Delays

Shipping times may vary based on destination and carrier conditions. Delays caused by customs or postal services are beyond our control.
