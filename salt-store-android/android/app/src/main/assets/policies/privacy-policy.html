This Privacy Policy explains how SALT collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you use our site or services.

What We Collect

  • Contact details such as name, address, phone number, and email.
  • Order information such as billing and shipping details, payment confirmation, and contact details.
  • Account information such as login details and security information.
  • Shopping information such as items viewed, saved, or purchased.
  • Customer support information that you choose to send us.

How We Use It

  • To provide products and services, process payments, fulfill orders, and manage your account.
  • To send shipping, returns, and account-related notifications.
  • To improve the shopping experience and tailor marketing and advertising.
  • To detect fraud, keep the service secure, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We may share personal information with service providers, payment processors, shipping partners, analytics providers, and other partners that help operate the store.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to access, delete, correct, or port your information, or manage communication preferences. You can opt out of promotional emails at any time.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or want to exercise your rights, email help@saltonlinestore.com or contact us at 317 Notre Dame Avenue, Dayton, OH, 45404, US.
