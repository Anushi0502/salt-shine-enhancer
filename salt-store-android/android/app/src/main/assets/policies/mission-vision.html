Mission & vision

SALT exists to make shopping feel clearer, calmer, and more useful from the first browse to checkout.

Mission

Offer a curated catalog of products that are practical, giftable, and easy to trust, while keeping support and policy information close to the buying experience.

Vision

Build a storefront people return to because it stays fresh, easy to navigate, and focused on products that improve everyday life.

How We Work

We prioritize clear product selection, value-forward pricing, simple shipping guidance, and a support path that stays inside the app.

What Matters Most

Thoughtful curation, transparent policies, and a shopping flow that feels steady instead of overwhelming.
